About HWC

AI Health & Wellness Companion

HWC is a wellness companion app for everyday self-care: sleep, activity, water, weight, and nutrition tracking, alongside an AI wellness assistant that can answer general questions and suggest healthy habits.

HWC is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. It does not provide medical advice, diagnoses, or prescriptions, and it will not recommend starting, stopping, or changing any medication. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions or concerns, and seek immediate care for anything urgent.

What HWC does

  • Lets you log and review your own sleep, activity, water, weight, and nutrition.
  • Computes a simple daily wellness summary from what you log.
  • Offers an AI chat assistant for general wellness questions and habit ideas.
  • Can estimate a rough nutrition breakdown from a food photo (an estimate, not a lab measurement).
  • Can give a best-effort, non-clinical summary of a health document photo you choose to upload — this is not medical-grade OCR or interpretation.

What HWC does not do

  • It does not replace a doctor, therapist, dietitian, or other licensed professional.
  • It does not interpret lab results or medical imaging in a clinically reliable way.
  • It does not handle medical emergencies — if you are experiencing one, contact local emergency services immediately.
